Set up a player phone

Cards

Before you start

  • Print the parent cards first (below). Once the phone is locked, they are the only way back into its settings, short of another factory reset.
  • Don't add a Google account to the old phone. Android refuses to set it up this way if it has one.
  • Everything on the old phone is erased.

Steps

  1. Charge the old phone.
  2. Factory reset it: Settings → System → Reset → Erase all data (on Samsung: General management → Reset).
  3. On the very first welcome screen, tap the same spot 6 times quickly. A QR code reader opens (older phones may ask for Wi-Fi first to download it).
  4. Scan the QR code below.
  5. Wait. The phone downloads Tap and Play, installs it and locks itself into it. This takes a few minutes.

Wi-Fi for the player

Optional. Without it, the phone asks for Wi-Fi during setup. What you type here only goes into the QR code, in this browser; it is never sent anywhere.

Parent cards

Two cards that only a grown-up should have. Tap one on the player (or hold up its QR code):

  • Settings: leaves the locked mode and opens Settings. Press Home to lock again.
  • Release: unlocks the phone for good, so the app can be uninstalled.

The PDF is one A4 sheet: print it double-sided, flip on long edge, at Actual size / 100%, and cut along the marks. Or write tapandplay://settings and tapandplay://release as URI records on two NFC stickers.
