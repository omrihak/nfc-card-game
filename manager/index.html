Type

Tapped on its own, a word card shows its picture and says the words. It is also what the games use. The phone speaks the text, so there is nothing to record.

For "Where is the dog?" in Hebrew the word often needs a different form (איפה הכלב?). Leave it empty to use the Hebrew word as is. You can add vowel marks (כֶּלֶב) if the voice mispronounces it.

      Print cards

      Tick the cards to print, 6 per A4 sheet. The PDF has two pages per sheet: pictures, then QR codes. Print it double-sided, flip on long edge, at Actual size / 100% (not "Fit to page"). Cut along the marks.

      Back side doesn't line up?

      Some printers shift the second side a little. Print one sheet, hold it up to a light and measure how far each QR code should move, looking at the QR side. Enter that here (negative for left / up); it is remembered.
